Product Requirements Document

Pothole Radar Operations Command Center

Version: 2.0.0 Status: Detailed Review Date: 2026-07-23

1. The Core Proposition

The Problem

Urban infrastructure is degrading, and potholes pose a severe threat to vehicle integrity and commuter safety. Currently, municipal reporting systems are outdated, bureaucratic, and lack transparency. Citizens feel ignored when they report hazards, and public works departments struggle to prioritize repairs efficiently due to disorganized data streams and lack of community feedback.

The Solution

A centralized, community-driven Pothole Radar Operations Command Center. This application bridges the gap between frustrated commuters and city administrators. By crowdsourcing pothole discovery with precise geolocation, severity ratings, and an upvoting mechanism, we create a transparent, real-time map of infrastructural decay. Municipalities gain an actionable dashboard to track, prioritize, and communicate repair statuses, ultimately building trust and making roads safer.

2. Targeted Markets & Geographies

While the platform is architected to scale globally, the initial rollout and targeted marketing will focus on countries with significant road infrastructure challenges, dense urban populations, and high commuter reliance on personal vehicles. The primary target markets include:

  • India: High volume of monsoon-related road degradation and a massive commuter base.
  • United States: Aging infrastructure across major metropolitan areas and extreme weather zones (e.g., snow belt states).
  • United Kingdom: Significant local council backlogs for road repairs and active citizen advocacy groups.
  • South Africa: Rapidly expanding road networks requiring localized community monitoring due to municipal resource constraints.

Localization (multi-language support and region-specific news feeds) will be tailored primarily for these regions in Phase 1.

3. Feature Highlight: Facts & Local News Integration

One of the standout modules of this application is the Educational & News Hub. It goes beyond mere reporting by fostering civic awareness.

  • Local News Alerts: Integration with public APIs to scrape and display local roadwork alerts, traffic diversions caused by infrastructure decay, and city budget announcements regarding road maintenance.
  • Pothole Facts & Statistics: A dynamic infographic section that displays the cost of vehicle damage caused by potholes annually, the lifecycle of a pothole (how weather creates them), and the time saved by community reporting.
  • Transparency Metrics: Real-time statistics showing how many potholes were reported vs. repaired in the last 30 days within the user's district, turning raw data into a narrative of municipal efficiency.

4. Core Architecture & Capabilities

🎯 Live Telemetry Map

A high-performance Leaflet map providing a real-time radar view of active potholes. Features clustering for dense areas and color-coded markers based on community-voted severity.

⚡ Frictionless Reporting

A streamlined submission pipeline. Users can drop a pin (or use GPS), snap a photo, and classify the hazard in under 30 seconds. No forced account creation for basic reporting to ensure high participation.

🛡️ Admin Command Center

A secure Firebase-authenticated terminal for city workers. Includes Kanban-style state management (Reported -> Surveyed -> Scheduled -> Fixed) and exportable repair manifests.

🔥 Community Upvoting

A Reddit-style upvote system for existing potholes. High-traffic hazards naturally bubble to the top of the municipality's priority list, ensuring public works addresses the most critical issues first.

5. Technical Blueprint

Built for resilience and speed, wrapped in an aggressive red-and-black aesthetic.

  • Frontend: React 19, Vite, TypeScript.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS v4, custom CSS variables for the dark/red theme.
  • Motion/UX: Framer Motion for dramatic page wipes and satisfying micro-interactions.
  • Geospatial: Leaflet & React-Leaflet.
  • Backend/Database: Express, Node.js, Firebase Auth & Firestore for real-time data sync.

🚀 Proposed Bonus Features (Expansion Pack)

To elevate this application from a standard utility to a cutting-edge platform, the following features are recommended for subsequent phases:

  • AI Damage Assessment (Gemini API): Users upload a photo of the pothole, and the server-side Gemini Vision model automatically estimates the depth, severity, and potential vehicle damage risk, auto-categorizing the report.
  • Gamification & Civic Leaderboards: "Pothole Hunter" badges and point systems for users whose reports lead to confirmed repairs. Top reporters get recognized on a civic leaderboard.
  • Before & After Sliders: When an admin marks a pothole as "Fixed," they upload a repair photo. The public map then displays a satisfying interactive before/after image slider for that location, proving municipal action.
  • Route Avoidance Integration: An exportable geo-JSON feed that can be integrated into Google Maps or Waze, warning drivers of high-density pothole zones before they hit them.
  • Automated Cost Estimator: For the Admin Dashboard, an algorithm that calculates the estimated asphalt required and repair cost based on the AI's size assessment of the reported potholes.

6. Non-Functional Requirements

  • Aesthetics: Strict adherence to the "Aggressive Dark" theme. Deep blacks (`#050505`), stark whites, and vibrant red (`#ef4444`) accents to convey urgency and action.
  • Performance: Map tiles must render instantly. Database queries for map markers must be spatially indexed so panning the map remains at 60fps even with thousands of data points.
  • Accessibility: High contrast text, screen-reader friendly forms, and keyboard navigable mapping tools.
